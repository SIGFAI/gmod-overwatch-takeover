# Overwatch Takeover

**Garry's Mod** × Overwatch · visual · idea by the team

Bring Overwatch into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned Garry's Mod into Overwatch: rogue robots vs heroes, a payload bathtub to escort, and my ultimate rained rockets for Play of the Game.

## Install

Unzip the mod folder into a new folder inside garrysmod/addons, start Sandbox on any map, and the robots and heroes appear. Press Q when your ultimate is READY.
